# Grounding notes

This prototype is based on the drafting concepts extracted from the attached Caedrel transcript in the conversation. It intentionally preserves the transcript's reasoning style rather than trying to reproduce full League mechanics.

Grounded examples used in the prototype:

- matchup reasoning through range / trading / push instead of a primitive "counter" label;
- picks generating response branches;
- protecting Jarvan consuming ban resources that could otherwise shape support;
- player matchup opinions changing whether a response must be banned;
- itemization changing matchup structure;
- Caitlyn/Lux vs Kalista/Nautilus turning lane requirements into jungle pathing obligations;
- two pushing lanes competing for jungle attention;
- weak-side resilience reducing cover requirements;
- mid push propagating into fog access and cross-map numbers.

The exact champion facts are illustrative and should be reviewed by domain experts before being treated as production knowledge.
